About SF Today

Who's building this, why it exists, and what's coming next.

Why I Built This

SF Today started because most "things to do in San Francisco" sites are written for tourists — the same handful of landmarks, recycled listicles, and zero sense of what's actually happening today. I wanted something I'd genuinely open every morning: live weather, real recurring markets, an honest street sweeping lookup so I stop getting tickets, and guides that tell locals apart from tourist traps instead of blurring them together.

It's built and maintained by one person, in spare time, as an ongoing project rather than a finished product.

What Makes It Different

No ads. No selling your data. No generic listicle content. SF Today pulls from real public data where it can — live weather, official street sweeping schedules, recurring farmers markets — and pairs it with guides across all 11 San Francisco districts that call out which spots are genuine local favorites versus tourist-heavy stops.

What's Coming

This is very much a work in progress. Recently added: "Spin the SF Wheel" daily pick, site-wide search, a "My SF Checklist" spot tracker, the neighborhood quiz, live sunset/golden hour times, daily trivia, and basic offline support. Still on the roadmap: deeper neighborhood-specific content, an actual events feed beyond farmers markets, and a live Muni alerts feed once it's fully wired up. Check the "See What's New" badge on the homepage for the latest.

Get Involved

Found a bug, have an idea, or just want to say something worked well? Send feedback — it goes straight to the person building this. If SF Today's helped you find something worth doing, you can also chip in to help keep it running.
