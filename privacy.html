Privacy Policy

Plain-language version: we don't run ads, we don't sell your data, and this page explains exactly what little we do collect.

Information We Collect

Email signup: Our weekly mailing list isn't fully connected yet. Until it is, signing up opens your own email app addressed to us instead — nothing is stored on our end automatically. Once a real mailing list provider is connected, this section will be updated to name it.

Feedback form: Name, email, a star rating, and/or a written message — all optional, and you choose what to share. Depending on how the site is currently configured, this either goes to a private inbox or opens your own email app to send directly; SF Today doesn't store submissions in a database.

Donations: Handled entirely by PayPal (or another processor we link out to). SF Today never sees or stores your card, bank, or payment details — that transaction happens on the payment provider's own site, governed by their privacy policy.

Location (optional): If you tap "Find My Nearest" under Explore by Neighborhood, your browser will ask permission to share your location. That coordinate is used only inside your own browser to calculate distance to each neighborhood — it's never sent to our servers, logged, or stored anywhere.

Cookies & Local Storage

A few small preferences are saved using your browser's local storage, not a tracking cookie — none of it is sent to us or anyone else, and all of it stays on your device:

  • Your dark mode preference
  • Your current streak and last-played date for the "Guess the SF Spot" game
  • Your "My SF Checklist" — which spots you've marked as visited, and your highest milestone reached

If your browser supports it, the site also caches its own static pages for offline use once you've visited — this uses your browser's built-in offline storage (a "service worker"), not a cookie, and only ever stores the site's own files, never your personal data.

Third-Party Services We Rely On

Some features pull from outside services to work: live weather (Open-Meteo), sunset and golden hour times (sunrise-sunset.org), street sweeping schedules (DataSF, San Francisco's public open data), and Muni service alerts (511.org, once connected). None of these send your personal information anywhere — they're used to fetch public, non-personal data like weather conditions, sunset times, and transit schedules.

What We Don't Do

No ads. No selling or renting your data to advertisers or anyone else. No third-party analytics or tracking scripts are currently enabled on this site. If that ever changes — for example, adding a privacy-friendly, cookie-free tool like Plausible or GoatCounter to see basic page views — this page will be updated first to say so.

Your Choices

You can unsubscribe from any email list at any time using the link in that email. To ask a question, request your information be deleted, or raise a concern, use the feedback page — it goes straight to the person running this site.

Changes to This Policy

As SF Today adds real features (a live mailing list, a proper feedback dashboard, etc.), this page will be updated to reflect exactly what's actually happening with your data — not what's planned.
