Frequently Asked Questions

What's the best day to visit the Ferry Building Farmers Market?

Saturday is the biggest, with 100+ vendors. Tuesday and Thursday have smaller, quieter versions of the same market if you want to avoid the crowds.

Is San Francisco safe to walk around at night?

Like any major city, it depends heavily on the neighborhood and block. Stick to busy, well-lit areas at night, use rideshare or taxis for longer trips after dark, and use the same street smarts you would in any big city.

How do I avoid a street sweeping parking ticket?

Use our Street Sweeping Lookup tool above to check your street's schedule, then always double check the posted sign on your exact block, since times can vary block to block.

Is San Francisco expensive?

Yes — it consistently ranks among the two or three most expensive cities in the US, though the exact ranking shifts depending on which cost-of-living study you look at. That said, there's a lot to do for free or cheap; check our Free Things to Do guide.

Why is San Francisco so foggy in the summer?

The fog, nicknamed "Karl the Fog" by locals, forms when warm inland air pulls cool, moist ocean air over the city. It's most common in summer. Bring layers, even in July.

Do I need a car in San Francisco?

Not really. Muni and BART cover most of the city, and many neighborhoods are very walkable. A car mostly becomes useful for trips outside the city.
