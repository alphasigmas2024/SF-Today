Release Notes

A running log of every update, feature, and tweak made to SF Today.

📅 November 20, 2026

Fixed — a real bug, diagnosed and confirmed
  • The "All day" planner option could return only a single generic fallback pick for some persona/budget combinations — diagnosed as a real data gap (only 7 of 18 activities were tagged for all-day plans). Added 25 new activities spanning every persona and every budget tier for both "a few hours" and "all day," and verified programmatically that all 40 persona/budget/time combinations now return at least one real match — zero gaps left, not just patched for the reported case.
Added
  • More One Direction achievements: corrected and expanded award totals (7 Brit Awards, 7 American Music Awards, 6 Billboard Music Awards, 6 Guinness World Records, 28/31 Teen Choice Awards), the "first three albums" and "first two albums" Billboard 200 milestones leading up to the four-for-four record, and their 2013 Forbes earnings ranking.
  • A sixth FAQ question on the One Direction page, matching the main site's FAQ style.

📅 November 16, 2026

Added
  • Real walking-directions links between stops on any generated plan — powered by Google Maps' own routing, not a guessed time.
  • The homepage's guide cards now quietly reorder around today's actual weather (outdoor guides first on clear days, indoor first on bad ones), with a small note explaining why.
  • A "⚠️ Report an issue" link on every venue card, pre-filling the feedback form with that venue's name.
  • "📸 Download as Image" on the neighborhood quiz result and the daily wheel spin — a real, branded, downloadable PNG generated in-browser.
  • A "✓ Checked [date]" line on every venue card, tied to the About page's honesty section about how a one-person site stays current.
  • Small print-stylesheet touch-up so the new report-issue links and reorder note never show up in a printed plan.

📅 November 13, 2026

Added
  • Big expansion to the One Direction page's achievements: the "5 for 5" UK Albums Chart record, full award totals (Brit, MTV VMA, MTV EMA), and the real 2024 streaming resurgence following Liam Payne's death — verified via search rather than reusing an old, unlabeled figure.
  • An FAQ section on the One Direction page.
  • The One Direction timeline is now linked from the homepage quick-nav, set visually apart with a divider since it's a different page, not a site section.

📅 November 9, 2026

Added
  • A standalone One Direction timeline page — unrelated to the SF guide by design, linked quietly from the footer.

📅 November 5, 2026

Fixed
  • Street Sweeping Lookup was pointed at DataSF's old domain (data.sfgov.org), which they migrated to data.sf.gov this month — updated to the new official domain before the old one stopped working.

📅 November 1, 2026

Fixed
  • Redesigned every favicon and app icon. The original bridge illustration had fine cable lines and cross-bracing that turned into illegible noise at real tab-icon size (16–32px) — genuinely looked broken, not just plain. Replaced with a bold "SF" monogram for small sizes and a monogram plus a simplified single-arch bridge accent for larger icons where there's room for it.

📅 October 28, 2026

Fixed
  • The quick-nav was silently broken: a duplicate CSS rule overwrote its sticky positioning, and an auto-scroll effect was calling scrollIntoView() in a way that fought the page's own vertical scroll. Both fixed — sticky positioning no longer gets overridden, and the active-link auto-scroll is now scoped strictly to the nav's own horizontal strip.
  • The quick-nav was also getting clipped mid-word on smaller screens with no indication it could scroll — added fade edges. The checklist button's icon and count ran together with no spacing — fixed.
Removed
  • SF Essentials — decided it wasn't earning its place.
  • "Today at a Glance" — it duplicated the header's weather and the full Sunset & Fog section below it. Removed the redundant middle layer.

📅 October 24, 2026

Fixed
  • The Spin the SF Wheel had two segments (History and Trivia) sharing the exact same 📜 icon — genuinely confusing on an 8-slice wheel. Gave History its own icon.
Added
  • A visible legend under the wheel explaining what each of the 8 categories means, instead of relying on icons alone.
  • A gentle idle wobble inviting a first spin — stops automatically once you've actually spun it.

📅 October 20, 2026

Added
  • A sticky "jump to" quick-nav bar for getting around the homepage's many sections faster.
  • Real loading skeletons in place of plain "Loading..." text for weather, Muni alerts, and sunset data.
  • A "skip to content" link and other small accessibility touches.
  • Expanded the planner's budget filter from 3 tiers to 5 (Free, Under $25, $25–$75, $75–$150, $150+).
  • Grew the site from 74 to 100 real, addressed places — Baker Beach, the Botanical Garden, the Japanese Tea Garden, Angel Island, the SF Zoo, and dozens more.

📅 October 16, 2026

Changed
  • Removed the "I'm on a Date" planner option and the Dating Ideas guide — replaced with a "📜 SF History" guide covering Angel Island, the Fillmore's displacement of its Black community, and the wartime incarceration of Japantown's Japanese American residents. Every fact verified against multiple sources before publishing.
  • "Guess the SF Spot" expanded from 5 puzzles to 19 — almost three weeks of variety before it repeats.

📅 October 12, 2026

Added
  • The Spin the SF Wheel now shows a live, ticking countdown to the next spin, anchored to San Francisco's real midnight — and unlocks automatically the moment it hits, no refresh needed.
  • Three real, verified SF dining institutions added (Tadich Grill, House of Prime Rib, Tosca Cafe), each checked for a currently-operating address before publishing.
  • A "How Accuracy Actually Works Here" section on the About page — an honest account of how a one-person site stays current.
Fixed
  • The daily game, wheel, and trivia previously reset on UTC time or the visitor's own timezone instead of San Francisco's actual midnight — now anchored to real SF time, site-wide.
Changed

  • Removed the SF Slang Glossary.
