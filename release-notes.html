Release Notes

A running log of every update, feature, and tweak made to SF Today.

Dates below are placeholders spread across a month for readability — swap in your real dates whenever you're ready.

📅 Day 30

Launch Ready
  • Real favicons, full app icon set, and a proper 1200×630 social share image — replacing the old placeholder favicon everywhere.
  • Security headers and one-click deploy configs added for Netlify and Vercel.
  • A full site-wide QA pass: every link checked, every page's HTML validated, every script tested for errors.
  • Simplified the codebase down to a single JavaScript file for easier maintenance.

📅 Day 28

Added
  • 🎡 "Spin the SF Wheel" — one spin a day, lands on a category (Eat, Free Thing, Outdoor, Date Idea, Hidden Gem, Neighborhood, Sight, or Trivia) and reveals a real, shareable pick.

📅 Day 25

Added — Personalization
  • "Which SF Neighborhood Are You?" — a 5-question quiz with a shareable result.
  • "Find My Nearest Neighborhood," using optional, privacy-respecting browser geolocation — nothing is ever sent to a server.
  • A toast notification when the weather clears up while you have the tab open.
  • Basic offline support — the site's core pages now load even without a connection, once you've visited before.
  • A time-of-day greeting in the header (morning / afternoon / evening).

📅 Day 22

Added — Discovery Tools
  • Site-wide Quick Find — press / or tap 🔍 to search every spot, neighborhood, slang term, and FAQ on the site.
  • "My SF Checklist" — mark any spot as visited anywhere on the site, and track progress toward exploring the whole guide, with milestone celebrations at 25/50/75/100%.
  • SF Essentials — a quick-reference card for non-emergency numbers, transit payment, tipping norms, and parking.
  • "What to Bring Today" — a packing list generated live from the weather and season.

📅 Day 20

Added — Live City Data
  • Live sunset & golden hour countdown, pulled from a public sunset API.
  • "Karl the Fog Meter" — a fun visual read on today's fog level.
  • SF Trivia of the Day, rotating daily.

📅 Day 18

Added — Plan & Share
  • Share and Print buttons on every generated plan.
  • "🎲 Surprise Me" button for instant random picks when you can't decide.
  • The planner now nudges toward outdoor or indoor picks based on the live weather.

📅 Day 16

Added — Smarter Planning
  • More planner filters: Indoor/Outdoor, a dog-friendly toggle, and an "I'm a Local" persona.
  • A much bigger activity database behind the planner to match against.
  • SF Today is now installable as a home-screen app on phones.

📅 Day 14

Added — Gamification Begins
  • Launched "Guess the SF Spot," a daily mystery-location game with a streak counter, rotating through five puzzles by day.
  • Confetti celebrations on real completed actions — winning the game, signing up, sending feedback, donating.
  • Native share-sheet support on mobile for sharing results.

📅 Day 11

Fixed — Honesty Pass
  • Email signup no longer falsely confirms success when no mailing list is connected — it now says so plainly and offers an email fallback.
  • Donate button no longer dead-ends on an unconfigured payment link — it now offers an email fallback to arrange a donation directly.
  • Muni Alerts shows an honest "coming soon" message instead of setup instructions meant for the site owner.
  • Corrected a typo in the "Is San Francisco expensive?" FAQ answer (and its structured data).
  • Unified the version number and footer text across every page.
  • This page was rebuilt to share the same design system, dark mode, and navigation as the rest of the site.
  • Added a proper 404 page.

📅 Day 8

Added — Community Features
  • Feedback page, with an optional star rating.
  • Donation page with preset and custom amounts.
  • Weekly email signup.

📅 Day 5

Added — Personality & Trust
  • SF Slang Glossary and a Frequently Asked Questions section.
  • About, Privacy Policy, and Terms of Service pages.
  • Dark mode, site-wide.

📅 Day 3

Added — Local Data Integration
  • Real-time street sweeping lookup, pulled from San Francisco's official public data.
  • "This Week in SF" — real recurring farmers markets, by day of the week.
  • "This Week's Local Favorite," a rotating hidden-gem pick.

📅 Day 1

Initial Launch
  • Launched the SF Today homepage with a custom "Build My Plan" filter (who you are, budget, time available).
  • Built 8 evergreen guide categories — eats, family activities, free things to do, and more.
  • Added guides for all 11 San Francisco neighborhoods.
  • Live SF clock and live weather in the header.
